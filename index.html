Everything runs locally in your browser. Nothing is recorded or uploaded — the video is only used to track your hand.

Neon draw

Point with only your index finger to paint a glowing light trail. Curl it back and the line lifts.

1 finger — Rose

In flower mode, hold up just your index finger to plant roses as you move.

2 fingers — Tulip

Index + middle extended plants tulips.

3 fingers — Sunflower

Index + middle + ring extended plants sunflowers. Make a fist any time to swap back to neon.

Works best in good lighting, hand upright, facing the camera, roughly arm's length away.
